💪 4-Week Programme

Week 1

Phase 1 — Establish Weight
▼

Push

▼
Chest
Machine Incline Chest Press3 × 8
Seated machine, handles at upper-chest height. Press forward and up. Targets upper chest.
Cable Chest Fly3 × 10
Cables at mid-height. Arms slightly bent, squeeze hands together in an arc in front of your chest.
Shoulders
Machine Shoulder Press3 × 8
Seated shoulder press machine. Press handles straight up.
Dumbbell Lateral Raises3 × 12
Standing, slight lean forward, raise DBs to the side until arms are parallel. Control the negative.
Triceps
Cable Tricep Extension (Rope)3 × 10
Rope on high cable. Elbows pinned to sides, push down and split the rope at the bottom.
Seated Tricep Push Downs3 × 10
Seated at cable machine, bar or rope. Push down from chest height. No momentum.
Core
Hanging Leg Raises4 × 12
Hang from a bar or captain's chair. Raise legs to 90°, slow on the way down.

Pull

▼
Back
Lat Pulldown3 × 8
Wide grip, pull bar to upper chest. Lean back slightly, squeeze shoulder blades.
Standing Cable Pullover3 × 10
High cable, straight bar. Stand facing machine, arms overhead. Pull bar down in arc to thighs — lats do the work.
Face Pulls3 × 12
Rope on high cable. Pull towards face, elbows high, hands beside ears. Rear delts & rotator cuff.
Biceps
Cable Bicep Curl3 × 10
Low cable, straight or EZ bar. Standard curl — elbows pinned to sides.
Hammer Curl3 × 10
Dumbbells with neutral grip (palms facing each other). Curl without rotating. Brachialis & forearms.
Core
Cable Twists4 × 12
Cable at chest height, stand side-on, arms extended, rotate torso away. Core does the work.
Dumbbell Leans4 × 12
Stand with DB in one hand, lean sideways, use obliques to pull upright. 12 each side.

Legs

▼
Quads
Hack Squat3 × 8
Machine — back against pad, feet shoulder-width. Push through heels. Quad-dominant.
Leg Extensions3 × 10
Seated machine. Full lockout, squeeze quads at top, slow negative.
Hamstrings
Lying Leg Curl3 × 10
Face-down on machine. Curl heels towards glutes.
Glutes
Glute Kickback Machine3 × 10
Standing or kneeling machine — drive leg back, squeeze glute at top. Alt: low cable with ankle strap.
Seated Hip Abduction3 × 12
Machine — push knees apart against pads. Outer glutes.
Calves
Seated Calf Raises3 × 15
Seated machine. Balls of feet on platform, push up, 1 sec hold, lower slowly.
Core
Machine Crunches4 × 12
Seated crunch machine. Ribs pull towards hips — not just nodding head.

Week 3

Phase 2 — New Exercises
▼

Push

▼
Chest
Seated Chest Press3 × 8
Machine — seated, push handles forward at chest height. Constant tension on chest.
Cable Crossovers3 × 10
Cables set HIGH. Step forward, pull handles down and together in front of hips. Lower/inner chest.
Shoulders
Standing Cable Shoulder Press3 × 8
Low cables, handle each hand at shoulder height. Press straight overhead. Core braced — no arching.
Cable Front Delt Raises3 × 12
Low cable behind you, single handle. Raise arm straight in front to eye height. One arm at a time.
Triceps
Cable Overhead Tricep Extension3 × 10
Face AWAY from high cable, rope behind head, elbows forward. Extend overhead. Like standing skull crushers.
Overhead Tricep Extension (DB)3 × 10
Standing, single DB behind head with both hands. Extend arms up without flaring elbows.
Core
Mat Core / Knee Ups4 × 12
Lie on mat, bring knees to chest, extend legs straight without touching floor. Lower back pressed down.

Pull

▼
Back
Pull-Ups (Weighted / Assisted)3 × 6
Overhand grip, full hang to chin over bar. Use belt for weight or assisted machine.
Seated Lat Pullover Machine3 × 10
Nautilus-style machine. Grip bar above head, pull down in arc to lap. Pure lat isolation.
Reverse Cable Fly3 × 12
Cables at face height, NO attachments. Grab left cable with right hand & vice versa. Pull apart. Rear delts.
Biceps
Double Cable Bicep Curl3 × 10
Stand between two high cables. Curl hands towards ears — front double bicep pose.
Rope Bicep Curl3 × 10
Low cable, rope attachment. Curl up and twist rope outward at top for peak contraction.
Core
Russian Twists4 × 12
Seated on floor, lean back 45°, feet off ground. Rotate plate or DB side to side. 12 each side.
Cable Woodchops4 × 12
High cable, pull diagonally from high to low across body in a chopping motion. 12 each side.

Legs

▼
Quads
Leg Press3 × 8
Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Push through whole foot. Don't lock knees.
Goblet Squat3 × 10
Hold DB or kettlebell at chest, squat deep. Keeps you upright — great for quads & glutes.
Hamstrings
Single Leg Curl3 × 10 ea
Lying or seated curl machine, one leg at a time. Fixes left/right imbalances.
Glutes
Cable Pull-Throughs3 × 10
Face AWAY from low cable, rope between legs. Hinge at hips, drive hips forward. Glutes & hamstrings.
Walking Lunges3 × 10 ea
Hold DBs, step forward into lunge, drive up into next step. Continuous walking.
Calves
Standing Calf Raises3 × 15
Smith machine or calf machine. Balls of feet on edge, push high, 2 sec hold at top.
Core
Hanging Leg Raises4 × 12
Hang from bar or captain's chair. Raise legs to 90°, slow on the way down.
